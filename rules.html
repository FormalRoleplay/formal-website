Server rules

Official server rules and expectations. Read before you play.

Mission Statement Formal Roleplay is built around serious, immersive roleplay with minimal restrictions on creativity. Players are encouraged to create unique stories, develop characters, and engage in meaningful interactions. Our goal is not to control roleplay, but to protect immersion and ensure everyone has a fair opportunity to enjoy their experience. If your actions negatively impact another player's ability to roleplay, you are likely violating the spirit of the server. Section 1: Community Expectations 1.1 Respect Others Treat all players with respect, both in and out of character. Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or targeted toxicity will not be tolerated. Constructive disagreements are fine; personal attacks are not. 1.2 Common Sense Staff cannot create a rule for every possible situation. Players are expected to use reasonable judgment and act in good faith. Exploiting loopholes in the rules may still result in punishment. 1.3 Staff Respect Follow staff instructions when issued. Do not argue administrative decisions during active situations. Appeals may be submitted through official channels. Section 2: Roleplay Standards 2.1 Stay In Character Remain in character at all times while in the city. Out-of-character discussions should be kept to approved channels. Breaking character during roleplay situations is prohibited. 2.2 No Immersion Breaking Immersion is the foundation of Formal Roleplay. Examples include: Mentioning Discord, FiveM, server scripts, admins, tickets, or game mechanics in character. Referring to players by their Discord or Steam names. Calling situations "RDM," "VDM," or "Fail RP" in character. Using information your character would not realistically know. 2.3 Value Your Life (NVL) Your character should reasonably fear injury, death, and consequences. Do not act unrealistically fearless during dangerous situations. Being held at gunpoint, outnumbered, or clearly disadvantaged should influence your decisions. 2.4 Fear RP Roleplay realistic reactions to threats and danger. Winning a situation is never more important than creating quality roleplay. 2.5 Character Consistency Maintain believable character behavior. Avoid constantly changing personalities, motives, or backgrounds without roleplay development. Section 3: Metagaming 3.1 Metagaming Metagaming is using information your character did not learn through roleplay. Examples: Using Discord streams to locate players. Watching Twitch streams to gain information. Relaying information while dead or unconscious. Using external communication to gain advantages. 3.2 Stream Sniping Stream sniping is strictly prohibited. Information obtained from streams may not be used in roleplay. Section 4: Powergaming 4.1 Powergaming Powergaming is forcing actions or outcomes onto another player without allowing reasonable response. Examples: Forcing injuries without roleplay. Instantly escaping restraints without roleplay. Unrealistic physical actions. 4.2 Unrealistic Actions Characters must operate within realistic human limitations. Avoid superhero behavior or unrealistic stunts. Section 5: New Life Rule (NLR) 5.1 Character Death When your character dies: You may not immediately return to the scene. You may not seek revenge based solely on the events that led to your death. You should treat the event as forgotten or significantly blurred. 5.2 Returning to Active Scenes Do not return to an active situation after being incapacitated or killed. Allow roleplay to naturally progress without re-entering. Section 6: Criminal Roleplay 6.1 Meaningful Crime Criminal activity should generate roleplay, not simply money. Create interactions, negotiations, investigations, and consequences. 6.2 Hostage Roleplay Hostages must be treated realistically. Hostage situations should provide meaningful roleplay for all parties involved. 6.3 Robberies Robberies must involve roleplay. Immediate violence without interaction is prohibited. 6.4 Escalation Major acts of violence should have reasonable roleplay escalation whenever possible. Section 7: Law Enforcement Roleplay 7.1 Professional Standards Law enforcement officers must maintain professional conduct. Abuse of police powers is prohibited. 7.2 Fair Play Officers should prioritize roleplay over winning. Criminals should prioritize roleplay over escaping at all costs. 7.3 Scene Continuation Arrests, investigations, and court proceedings should contribute to ongoing stories. Section 8: Vehicle Roleplay 8.1 Realistic Driving Drive in a manner appropriate for the situation. Constant reckless driving without roleplay justification is prohibited. 8.2 Vehicle Value Treat vehicles as valuable property. Do not intentionally destroy vehicles without roleplay reasoning. 8.3 Pit Maneuvers & Pursuits Use realistic driving standards during pursuits. Winning a chase should never take priority over quality roleplay. Section 9: Combat Rules 9.1 No Random Deathmatch (RDM) Attacking or killing another player without valid roleplay is prohibited. 9.2 No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM) Using vehicles as weapons without roleplay justification is prohibited. 9.3 Roleplay Before Violence Violence should generally follow roleplay escalation unless immediate danger exists. 9.4 Win Mentality Roleplay is not a competition. Prioritizing victory over storytelling may result in administrative action. Section 10: Character Development 10.1 Create Stories Characters should have goals, relationships, strengths, and weaknesses. Storytelling is encouraged over grinding money or collecting assets. 10.2 Consequences Matter Actions should have consequences. Characters should remember events and evolve through experiences. Section 11: Exploits & Abuse 11.1 Exploiting Exploiting bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics is prohibited. Report issues instead of abusing them. 11.2 Combat Logging Disconnecting to avoid roleplay is prohibited. 11.3 AFK Abuse Abusing AFK systems or automated mechanics is prohibited. Section 12: OOC Conduct 12.1 Keep OOC Separate Personal disputes should never affect roleplay. Do not retaliate against players because of OOC disagreements. 12.2 Scene Respect Do not interrupt active roleplay scenes. Avoid unnecessary third-party interference unless it makes sense for your character. 12.3 Reporting Issues If a rule is broken, finish the scene whenever possible. Submit reports after roleplay concludes rather than disrupting the experience. Formal Roleplay Golden Rule Immersion Comes First. If you are ever unsure whether something is allowed, ask yourself: "Would this action improve the roleplay experience for everyone involved, or would it break immersion and reduce the quality of the scene?" If it harms immersion, don't do it. Story over winning. Character over ego. Roleplay over mechanics.
